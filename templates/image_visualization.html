Getting a diagram worth keeping

Three images are drawn per topic, so ask for something a picture can actually carry.

  • Name a structure or a flow, such as "encoder-decoder attention" or "how a gradient moves back through two layers", rather than a definition.
  • Say the shape you want: architecture, decision boundary, data flow, before and after.
  • Pick a simpler detail level for something you will redraw by hand, a technical one for a figure closer to a textbook.
  • If a run misses, reword the topic and generate again. Each run takes about half a minute.
An image model draws text as shapes, so labels, axis numbers and formulas in these diagrams are often wrong even when the layout is right. Read the explanation for the facts and treat the picture as a sketch of the structure.
