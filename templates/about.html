About GyanGuru

Your AI-powered companion for mastering Machine Learning

Our Mission

GyanGuru is designed to make Machine Learning education accessible, interactive, and engaging for everyone. Whether you're a beginner taking your first steps into ML or an experienced practitioner looking to explore new concepts, our platform provides personalized learning experiences tailored to your needs.

Key Features

Text Explanations

Get comprehensive, well-structured explanations of any ML concept with examples and real-world applications.

Code Generation

Receive working Python implementations with detailed comments, ready to run in your environment.

Audio Learning

Listen to AI-generated audio explanations perfect for learning on the go or during your commute.

Visual Diagrams

See complex concepts visualized through AI-generated diagrams and technical illustrations.
