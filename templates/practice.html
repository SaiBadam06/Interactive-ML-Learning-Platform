Using this to revise

The questions come from a language model reading nothing but your topic and level, so they test recall of the mainstream account of an idea, not a syllabus.

  • Answer before reading anything. What you get wrong is the part worth studying.
  • Open a term chip to see its definition when a question turns on a word you half know.
  • Generate another set on the same topic for different questions.
  • Download the set to hand out or revise from later; the answers are at the end of the file.
A wrong answer key is possible. If a question and its explanation disagree with your course notes, trust the notes and move on.
